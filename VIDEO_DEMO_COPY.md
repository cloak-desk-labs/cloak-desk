## Usage Notes

- Keep copy under 5-7 words for maximum impact
- Use action verbs (protect, secure, obfuscate, analyze)
- Emphasize speed (seconds, instantly, real-time)
- Focus on benefits (privacy, anonymity, security)
- Match the energy and style of "Stunning imagery just in seconds"
