Your wallet exposes more than you realize. Patterns in your transactions, habits in your timing, strategies in your gas fees—all of it can be traced.On-chain privacy isn't about vanishing; it's about deciding what gets revealed. That's why we created CloakDesk: a layer for crypto users who want real control.

2/
Every address leaves a trail. Most people have no idea what their own reveals.CloakDesk scans your wallet end-to-end for privacy risks. It flags where you're most exposed—like repeating the same DEX, trading at consistent times, or sticking to predictable gas settings. You get a score, but the real value is the breakdown: it shows exactly what an outsider could piece together from your activity.

3/
Analysis is just the start. CloakDesk also handles the fixes.It routes transactions via relayers and mixers that obscure the origin. To anyone watching, the sender, recipient, and flow blend into the background noise. You adjust the level of mixing based on what you're doing—no all-or-nothing setup.

4/
Instead of total concealment, we focus on diffusion.Shadow wallets operate alongside your main one, simulating unrelated activity that overlaps with your actual moves. Observers see a stream of transactions, but sorting real from decoy becomes a guessing game.It's deliberate disorder to muddy the waters.

5/
None of this works without solid security.Our MPC vault distributes your private key across devices or trusted parties. No one piece holds the full picture, and you set the approval threshold yourself.Privacy here relies on math, not blind faith in a service.

6/
There are times when you need to verify details without handing over the full story.CloakDesk integrates zero-knowledge proofs for that: confirm KYC status, asset balances, or project contributions without leaking the underlying data.Proof without the giveaway.

7/
You can't manage what you can't see, so we added mapping tools.CloakDesk visualizes your transaction graph, highlighting connections across addresses and chains. A quick look often reveals how tightly linked your activity really is—fewer hops than you'd expect.

8/
Relayers keep things distributed by design.Pick from a network of independent nodes, each with its own reliability, costs, and supported chains. Swap providers mid-stream if needed. No central gatekeeper calling the shots.

9/
Privacy evolves with use—it's not a set-it-and-forget-it feature.Your CloakDesk score updates as you interact with the chain, reflecting shifts in your habits. It logs progress over time, helping you spot patterns to refine, not for points, but for sharper awareness.1

Perfect anonymity isn't feasible on a transparent ledger like this.CloakDesk doesn't promise the impossible. It equips you to complicate tracking, fragment insights, and shrink your visible profile.Tools to audit your exposure, reroute your flows, and own the data you leave behind.Your wallet, your traces.
