## 1. Privacy Health

**Title:** Privacy Health  
**Subtitle:** Analyze your wallet's privacy exposure and get actionable insights

**3D Graphic Prompt:**
```
Create a 3D crypto tech visualization of a privacy health shield icon. 
Show a pitch black background (#000000) with a 3D shield icon floating in the center. 
The shield should be rendered in 3D with depth, metallic/blockchain texture, and glowing edges in cyan (#06b6d4) transitioning to violet (#7c3aed). 
Include circuit board patterns etched into the shield surface with neon cyan and purple accents. 
Show floating blockchain data particles and cryptographic symbols around the shield. 
Add volumetric lighting with cyan and purple glows creating depth. 
The shield should have a metallic, high-tech crypto aesthetic with subtle reflections. 
Include subtle grid lines in the background for depth. 
The overall style should be 3D crypto tech - like a blockchain security icon rendered in 3D space.
```

## Color Palette Reference

- **Primary Start (Cyan/Teal):** #06b6d4
- **Primary End (Violet/Purple):** #7c3aed
- **Electric Purple:** #8b5cf6
- **Background Pitch Black:** #000000
- **Background Dark:** #0b0f14
- **Background Medium:** #0f1720
- **Background Light:** #1a2332

## Style Guidelines

- **Aesthetic:** 3D crypto tech, blockchain-inspired, futuristic
- **Background:** Pitch black (#000000) for all graphics
- **Effects:** Volumetric lighting, neon glows, holographic elements, particle effects
- **Mood:** Secure, private, cutting-edge, mysterious, high-tech
- **Visual Elements:** 3D geometric shapes (hexagons, cubes, dodecahedrons), metallic blockchain textures, circuit boards, grids, data streams
- **Lighting:** Volumetric lighting, neon accents, dramatic shadows, depth perception
- **3D Elements:** All icons and elements should be rendered in 3D space with depth, dimension, and metallic textures
- **Crypto Tech Style:** Blockchain-inspired textures, cryptographic patterns, distributed network aesthetics
